The safety covenant

Why it exists.

Read before use

“The default answer is always: Unidentified — do not ingest.”

TrailSnack is an educational reference, not a permission slip. The app never declares any wild find safe. On-device AI only ranks your photo against a bundled database; a deterministic safety engine then applies worst-case rules, where toxic lookalikes always win. Confirm everything with a qualified local expert before touching or tasting anything wild.

  • Worst case wins
  • No app confirms mushrooms
  • AI can only lower a verdict
